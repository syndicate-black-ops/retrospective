SBO 1.38 Retrospective

A foreword from MrCalvin · October 26, 2026

I first played Syndicate Black Ops around 2005. I remember being in awe of it: all the weapon choices, the brutal difficulty, and the weird broken English that littered the game's menus, textures and text messages. I've been a Half-Life enthusiast since 2002 and have played hundreds of mods, popular and obscure, and SBO was like nothing I had seen before.

I forgot about SBO until 2021, when I decided to run a server for it alongside my other Half-Life servers. I gave up after a couple of days: no matter what I did, SBO would not list itself on the master server, an odd defeat given my background in networking. I didn't try again until the 25th Anniversary update for Half-Life came out at the end of 2023. Shortly after, I saw that Boxyt was organizing SBO game nights through a Steam group. I wanted to give people a convenient place to play, and I had never actually played SBO with other people, so I was determined to solve it once and for all.

Since then, running these servers as a hobby, I have slowly chipped away at the game. At first it was a few fixes to improve the experience, but over time every feature, bug and idea became a new challenge. In the background, Boxyt and I tried to recover SBO's source from the original developers (which we could not get, as it may have been lost to time), while I learned reverse engineering and decompilation to do it myself, something I achieved two years later. Much of this work is invisible or easily forgotten (most players would assume it is simply how the game shipped, not something I had to go out of my way to add), so I made this page to show what went into it.

With SBO 2.0.0, everyone gets what was once only available on my servers. I don't plan to stop running them any time soon, but a stable SBO no longer depends on me: the crash fixes, bug fixes and stability work will now ship with the game itself. If I ever lose interest or can no longer dedicate the time, people can still play this weird, weird mod on their own, with every fix included.

Thanks to everyone for playing. Here's to SBO 2.0.0 and beyond

Game client patch

Client freeze fix

What
A 22-byte patch to SBO's client.dll that sets the camera angles before the game uses them
Why
A routine Steam update in April 2026, not a Half-Life update, woke up a bug that had sat dormant in SBO's client for years: it froze in online play, which made SBO unplayable online
When
Apr 30, 2026
Where
Players' game

The original code never set the camera's angles before tracing the camera's position, so it traced with leftover memory. For years that went unnoticed, until a routine update to Steam itself in April 2026 brought the dormant bug to life: the trace now ran into an endless loop and the game froze. As far as I know no other mod is affected; I tested around 30 popular and obscure mods after the bug appeared. The patch sets the angles to zero in the same 22 bytes

Game bugs, fixed from outside

SBO's game code was never released, so I traced each bug in the binary and patched it from outside

Server crashes fixed

  • Pitbulls could crash the whole server: the pitch of their bark kept dropping until it went below zero
  • A grenade that exploded after the player who threw it had left the game
  • A tripmine placed on something that moves, such as a door or a platform
  • Dark grunts and imps attacking an enemy that had already been removed from the game
  • On co_appart and co_impappart, a broken collision surface in the bathtub water crashed the server with SV_RecursiveHull errors

Player crashes and disconnects

  • co_carnage crashed players' games, because of how the mod handled its particle effects
  • Zombie grunts crashed players' games: SBO's zombie grunt model still pointed at the human grunt's animation files, built for a 25-bone skeleton, and played them on its own 32-bone skeleton until its bones spazzed out and the game crashed. Fixed on the server, with fixed model files for your own game on GameBanana
  • Players were randomly disconnected with an SVC_BAD error, a broken network message; fixed

Rounds and chat

  • If everyone left mid-round, the round counter kept going up on the empty server, so the next players joined on a high round. A bug in SBO's round counting also made the first round of every map round 2. Both fixed: every map now starts at round 1
  • A player rejoining mid-round could break the round and strip players of their weapons; fixed
  • Chat messages often went missing, so players didn't see each other's messages or even their own. This is far rarer now: not gone completely, but nowhere near as widespread

On screen

  • Tank rockets and turrets didn't fire at all once a match had more than one player: they kept switching between every player on the server, usually to one out of sight, so they never locked on to anyone. Now each picks the nearest player it can see and keeps that target while it stays in view
  • Minos: added his gun's shell casing models, which the SBO team forgot to ship. He no longer locks on to a Fed in a far-off part of the map, through walls, while ignoring the players in front of him; he now fights whoever he can see
  • When a Sectum Streuoum died, its attack beam stayed hanging in the air; it now goes away
  • A map's sky lighting carried over into the next map, so NPCs, weapons and other studio models were lit wrong there; fixed
  • Old vote menus stayed on screen after a map change; fixed. Some menus were also squished by a bug in the 25th Anniversary update for Half-Life, which you all but need to play on my servers because of their higher limits; fixed as well

Metamod module

Unprecacher

What
Stops the server loading models, sprites and sounds a map never uses, and swaps or merges others to free up room
Why
GoldSrc has room for 511 models per map, and in GoldSrc sprites and brush entities (doors, lifts, breakables, trigger areas) are models too, not just studio models (NPCs, weapons, props). My extra content needs slots as well, so big custom maps crashed on my server, and the bigger the map, the more plugins and features I had to cut to play it. With the Unprecacher, neither side has to give
When
Since Feb 2026
Where
US/EU
  • Works out which files a map loads but never uses, and skips them
  • Rules per map to remove, replace or keep single files
  • Pairs with a plugin that packs the 40 weapon models you see on other players and on the floor (p_ and w_ models) into one model, freeing 39 slots on every map

SBO 1.38, decompiled · the foundation for SBO 2.0.0

A 1:1, hash-matching decompilation

What
I decompiled SBO 1.38's compiled game files back into source code: the client, the Windows server and the Linux server, with the original function names, in the original order and layout the compilers and linkers placed them in
Why
SBO's source code was never released. A source that matches the original exactly means SBO 2.0.0 starts from the real 1.38, every behavior and every bug included, instead of a guess at how the game worked. Fixes can now be made in the game itself instead of patched in from outside
When
Worked on continuously for more than two years; the full match was confirmed on October 10, 2026
How we know
Compiled with the same compilers SBO was built with in its day, the source produces files that are byte-for-byte identical to the ones in the official 1.38 installer. Their hashes, a fingerprint where changing a single byte changes the result, are exactly the same, confirmed by an automated build once the match was complete
